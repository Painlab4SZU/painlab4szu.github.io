PAIN RESEARCH AT SHENZHEN UNIVERSITY

Understanding pain.
Connecting minds.

We study how the brain gives rise to pain, how pain can be modulated, and how we share the experiences of others. Our work brings together psychophysics, electrophysiology, and non-invasive neuromodulation.

Dynamic expectation strength and precision shape human pain perception through shared and dissociable α-oscillatory mechanisms

How do evolving expectations shape pain? This study combines computational modeling and EEG to examine the roles of expectation strength, precision, and alpha oscillations.

01 /

Pain perception & neural dynamics

Investigating how expectations and attention shape pain, with a focus on brain oscillations, evoked responses, and dynamic brain states.

02 /

Neuromodulation & pain regulation

Examining the mechanisms of pain modulation through transcranial stimulation, peripheral electrical stimulation, and EEG neurofeedback.

03 /

Empathy & interpersonal connection

Exploring how we understand and share others’ experiences, and how interpersonal context relates to empathy and brain-to-brain synchronization.
